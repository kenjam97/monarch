# Study sequence

Open `http://127.0.0.1:4317/study-film.html` while Monarch is running.

This is the approved sequence used on the app's Study page. The standalone URL remains available as a visual reference. The synthetic hand does not write to the database.

The hand runs for approximately 23 seconds, then stops at the river decision. Choose fold, call or raise to see the recorded outcome. The opening can be replayed from Menu. The timeline and street names can be used to move through the hand. Space pauses playback. Camera motion can be disabled in Menu. An operating-system reduced-motion preference opens directly at the decision.

The sequence uses the existing synthetic `DEMO-RIVER-001` hand. Its states are generated by the app's parser and replay engine with `work/film-data.ts` in the workspace. At the decision the pot is 43.5 BB, the call is 18 BB and the break-even equity is 29.3%. The opponent's cards and later actions are hidden until the recorded outcome is shown.

The table, card faces, backs and chips are original procedural geometry and canvas drawings. Three.js and Libre Caslon Display are vendored locally with their licenses. The preview does not load film assets, remote fonts or third-party scripts.

The composition uses full-frame table views, a 3.5-second split-screen pan across all three flop cards, mixed-case Caslon titles and plain text for amounts. Turn and river camera moves ease continuously between positions. The entire board is fitted into the available space at each window shape. The desktop decision uses a paper panel beside the table. Mobile moves that panel below the table. Whites remain neutral; Bordeaux sits in the felt and printed suits rather than a warm overlay.

Chip geometry is instanced, shadows are cached between object movements and the canvas backing surface stays stable during the decision reveal. The `?timing` development query shows measured frame intervals during playback. `work/check-film-camera.mjs` checks content bounds at five window sizes, continuity at street transitions and the complete flop pan.

Browser checks covered the opening, street views, automatic river stop, pause, timeline, menu, motion switch, hidden action history, automatic recorded outcome and the 390 × 844 mobile layout. A fresh build and JavaScript syntax check passed.

`mountStudyFilm` mounts the same sequence into a scoped DOM inside the app. The app Menu pauses it. Navigating away releases its event handlers, animation frame, observers and GPU resources. Card faces come from `art.js`, shared with `TableRoom` and the compact card displays.
